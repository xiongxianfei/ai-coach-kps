## Target and reference relation

Target: compare two quantities on a common basis and justify the calculation. The constructed rule is unit cost = price / quantity, with quantities expressed in the same units. No external shopping decision or financial recommendation is being made.

For A, $6 divided by 750 g is $0.008 per g, or $0.80 per 100 g. For B, $7.20 divided by 1,000 g is $0.0072 per g, or $0.72 per 100 g. Total price alone does not answer this constructed per-unit question.

## From subject panorama to one first task

A **constructed panorama** locates unit-price reasoning in quantitative reasoning: arithmetic, measurement and units, proportional relationships and data interpretation. The current goal is one narrower relation—compare amounts on a common quantity basis—rather than mastering those entire regions. The [two-level planning Practice](../practices/concept-mapping-and-instructional-sequencing-with-ai.md) proposes conversion, unit rate and integrated comparison as *possible* learning stages. For the first learning task, it selects only one output:

| Task card field | Constructed first task |
|---|---|
| Goal | Recognize equivalent quantities so a later rate comparison can use common units |
| Task | Explain whether 0.75 kg and 750 g denote the same quantity, using the declared unit relation |
| Observable output | A short explanation of equivalence in your own words |
| Criterion | Explicitly identify that 1 kg equals 1000 g and the value of the quantity is preserved |
| Allowed support | An accessible text display; verified explanation on request is recorded as instructional assistance |
| Stop or split | After one response and checking, proceed to a rate task, teach the missing unit relation, or pause |

This is a proposed Day 1 *example*, not an actual day, interview, test or observed learning result. It is small without being mere disconnected vocabulary. The broader plan may have other stages, but they are not silently assigned as additional Day 1 requirements. See [One Learning Task at a Time](LEARNING-WORKFLOW.md).

## Coaching choices

| Point in a proposed session | Authored action | What must not be inferred |
|---|---|---|
| Learner chooses the target | Ask for an explanation of the quantity basis | A goal statement is not prior competence |
| An attempt is available | Preserve it and any hints | A corrected replacement is not the original |
| The relation is unclear | Offer a common-unit table or worked analogous example | A hint proves neither diagnosis nor effectiveness |
| A new response is produced | Check calculation and justification | One success is not general mastery |
| A later occasion is planned | Record intended interval separately | A plan is not retained learning |
