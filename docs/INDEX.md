## Concepts

- [Concept Map](../concepts/concept-map.md) — A concept map makes relationships explicit rather than merely listing topics.
- [Feedback](../concepts/feedback.md) — Feedback supplies information about performance or understanding; delivery alone does not establish improvement.
- [Formative Assessment](../concepts/formative-assessment.md) — Assessment becomes formative through its use in decisions about learning, not merely through collecting a score.
- [Instructional Scaffolding](../concepts/instructional-scaffolding.md) — Scaffolding supports task components the learner cannot yet manage, with support adjusted to the task and evidence.
- [Learning–Performance Distinction](../concepts/learning-performance-distinction.md) — Performance observed during assistance is not interchangeable with durable learning.
- [Metacognitive Calibration](../concepts/metacognitive-calibration.md) — Calibration compares a metacognitive judgment with a stated criterion, not with the fluency of an explanation.
- [Self-Regulated Learning](../concepts/self-regulated-learning.md) — Self-regulated learning includes monitoring and control of learning as well as motivational, affective and behavioral dimensions.

## Principles

- [Self-Explanation Effect](../principles/self-explanation-effect.md) — Learner-generated explanation can support understanding under studied conditions.
- [Spacing Effect](../principles/spacing-effect.md) — Learning episodes separated in time can affect later retention, with useful gaps depending on the retention goal.
- [Testing Effect](../principles/testing-effect.md) — Retrieval can contribute to later retention rather than only measure current recall.

## Models

- [Assessment and Feedback](../models/assessment-and-feedback.md) — A response becomes useful evidence when it is interpreted against a clear capability and criterion; feedback connects that interpretation to something the learner can understand and revise.
- [Assessment Triangle](../models/assessment-triangle.md) — Assessment connects a model of cognition, observations and their interpretation.
- [Cognitive Apprenticeship](../models/cognitive-apprenticeship.md) — The published framework relates contextual expertise, guided participation and increasingly independent activity.
- [Instruction and Scaffolding](../models/instruction-and-scaffolding.md) — Explain enough connected knowledge for the learner to understand what happens, how, why and when; make the relevant reasoning visible, then give the learner an opportunity to use it. Adjust support from the actual response.
- [Instructional Design](../models/instructional-design.md) — A learning design connects the intended capability, the activity and the evidence of success. Its route is a proposal informed by the learner and revised when actual responses or circumstances justify change.
- [Knowledge Representation](../models/knowledge-representation.md) — A useful representation makes the subject's important relationships explicit while preserving their meaning, scope and uncertainty. Its organization helps someone reason about a question; it does not by itself establish a teaching order or show what a learner knows.

## Methods

- [Cognitive Modeling](../methods/cognitive-modeling.md) — Make a knowledgeable task demonstration and its explanatory decisions available for a learner to inspect.
- [Concept Mapping](../methods/concept-mapping.md) — Construct and revise propositions that answer a defined focus question.
- [Retrieval Practice](../methods/retrieval-practice.md) — Attempt to retrieve encountered material before returning to it.
- [Self-Explanation](../methods/self-explanation.md) — The learner generates an explanation connecting studied information to its meaning or justification.
