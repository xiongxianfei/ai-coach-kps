## Conflicting and indirect evidence

The retrieval superiority claim is qualified by Mayrhofer and colleagues' altered comparison and outcome limitations. Adult college physics findings and adolescent mathematics findings differ in settings, controls and timing. The source descriptions retain these distinctions rather than average them into one AI-coaching effectiveness score. Teacher-informed solutions and platform guardrails are not equivalent to an instruction in a chat.
