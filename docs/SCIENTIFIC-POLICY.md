# Scientific scope and source alignment

## Key takeaway

Keep evidence-based scientific meaning separate from authored AI coaching decisions and learner outcomes.

## Summary

This document combines the domain scope, academic source-alignment policy, adult learner boundaries, and evidence limits. The five KPS file roles remain an authoring convention, not an educational theory. Original coaching workflows belong in Practices.

## Academic source alignment policy

The following source relationships govern independently reusable scientific objects; the authored AI coaching workflows remain separate.

## Admission by role

| Role | Admission basis | Not sufficient |
|---|---|---|
| Concept | Located scholarly characterization or definition | A fashionable term with an unrelated citation |
| Principle | Scoped empirical result or supported methodological relationship | A normative coaching rule written as a declaration |
| Model | A faithful subset of a published representation, or an explicitly authored explanatory synthesis with located source contributions and limits | An unsupported theory, an original workflow disguised as a scientific model, or implied endorsement by cited authors |
| Method | A recognizable published operation and inspected procedural basis | An original prompt chain presented as a validated intervention |
| Practice | Explicit KPS application synthesis with usable actions | A list of links or a claim that it was empirically tested |

Original coaching workflows remain in Practices. Local prompt contracts, aid categories and record schemas are specifications or Practice content. They are not scientific Principles. A domain Model may integrate source-grounded explanations without claiming that the integrated representation was published or empirically validated as a whole.

## Domain Models and published frameworks

A knowledge domain groups related explanatory questions; it need not be an independently functioning component. Aim for one self-contained domain Model that explains the essential concepts, mechanisms, relationships, examples, boundaries and interfaces. Let the subject determine the headings and depth. A coverage gap does not require a new file for every topic.

Published frameworks retain their names and meanings. A broader domain Model can explain their relevant contributions locally and link to them for depth. Preserve useful supporting Concepts, Principles and Methods; neither a citation list nor a collection of links supplies the domain explanation.

For an authored explanatory synthesis, `basis_kind` must identify it as KPS-authored and a synthesis. Each source alignment uses `synthesis-contribution`, with a real Reference ID, inspected passage locator and a nonempty `contribution` describing the supported part. Explain these contributions visibly in Source alignment. Separate source-supported claims from the connective representation, constructed examples and application choices authored here. No minimum source count, fixed content quota or compulsory learning sequence is implied.

The integrated account remains provisional. Clearly state its limits, disagreements or unresolved mechanisms where relevant. Prompts and decisions for conducting a coaching session belong in Practices even when a Model explains why those decisions matter. This local admission policy extends the domain profile; it does not change the pinned Core validator, the five KPS roles or upstream source files.

## Alignment records

Each non-Practice object declares source_alignment with a Reference ID, a documentation relation and an actual passage locator. Allowed relations are definition-paraphrase, scoped-empirical-finding, methodological-relationship, published-model-subset, published-procedure-summary and, for Models only, synthesis-contribution. The published-model-subset relation describes a faithful academic-framework subset; synthesis-contribution identifies support for part of our integration, not publication of the whole. These are domain profile declarations, not silent equivalence to Core's standard-model-subset relation.

These declarations do not express formal ontology equivalence. Named sources support meanings only to their inspected extent. Source-defined scope takes precedence over local paraphrase.

## Academic naming

Use source-grounded kebab-case paths and recognized names where faithful. Preserve related-but-different meanings: retrieval is not spacing; metacognition is not all self-regulated learning; self-explanation is generated by the learner rather than supplied by AI. Academic hyphens can remain in canonical_name and prose; display headings follow the conservative Markdown profile.

Pieschl, not Schraw, authored the inspected calibration paper. A generic instruction loop is not Zimmerman's cyclical model. The latter is not included because an original source was not inspected sufficiently for admission.

## Evidence and originality

Each source record states publication identity, access extent, locators, contribution and nonclaims. A theoretical or review source can define a concept without proving an intervention effect. All AI adaptations and prompt wording remain provisional. Normative commitments to consent, privacy and access are policy choices, not invented empirical findings.

## Tests and review

The domain tests check declarations, targets, role relations and explicit limits. They cannot establish faithful paraphrase, adequate evidence or actual learning. No automated promotion to reviewed status is allowed in this draft.

## Independent adult learning scope

The adult learner retains authority over goals and assistance, while scientific claims remain provisional and subject to source applicability.

## Included work

Conceptual understanding, safe procedural learning, learner-generated explanation, retrieval, feedback, reflection and evaluation. Subject expertise remains necessary even though the workflow is domain-independent. Applications can use reading, mathematics or coding, but this package does not automatically validate the subject material.

New-subject coaching starts with a compact panoramic orientation, followed by a goal-specific concept map and an adaptive instructional sequence. Returning learners may reuse a checked overview. Panoramic coverage need not be exhaustive, and a conceptual relation is not a proven learning prerequisite. Planning status, source support and learner-performance evidence remain separate.

## Master orchestration remains an authored Practice

The sixth Practice coordinates an overall target, learner-specific stages, coherent lessons and current assessment activities. It contains inline planning, teaching, questioning, assessment, retrieval and adaptation procedures. The five specialists remain self-contained and keep their identities. `composes` declares maintenance relationships, not runtime execution or empirical validation; no composed child contains the master recursively.

The complete programme workflow is KPS-authored, not a newly established theory of Self-Regulated Learning. Academic provenance does not guarantee that the AI follows a prompt. The master must work without opening repository links; subject truth and appropriate assessment still need credible content and, where necessary, qualified judgment.

## Coherent lessons and flexible pacing

For new learning, the teaching unit is a coherent lesson capability with connected knowledge and observable assessment criteria. The authored default is a connected explanation, a clarification opportunity, a few short complementary questions, actual responses and concise combined feedback followed by the next lesson or a necessary repair. Routine continuation reuses existing agreement; a blocking gap, requested pause, scope change or completed goal changes that action. Each learning-stage boundary receives a connected summary with actual evidence, open gaps and the next stage, using one checked overview picture by default. Learner preferences and access needs can change the teaching mode or summary representation. Examples and templates illustrate choices rather than requiring their sequence or techniques; accuracy, actual-response preservation and evidence boundaries remain necessary. Learner summaries stay in a private learning record; reusable subject knowledge stays in its owning project. These pacing and summary choices are authored policy, not findings establishing an optimal question count, automatic mastery or a guaranteed benefit from images. Guided questions remain available when requested or needed; no universal explanation length or question frequency is claimed. Lesson scope and interaction pace are separate decisions. A bounded diagnostic or retrieval task can stand alone. The panoramic overview should locate that goal without becoming a mandatory comprehensive lecture. Day labels and study durations are adjustable scheduling decisions, not KPS Practice stages or universal learning laws. Sometimes an integrative task is needed; decomposition must preserve meaning. See [the next-task guide](../guides/learning-workflow.md).

## Roles and consent

The AI proposes tasks, explanations, hints or feedback; the adult chooses whether to use them. The learner may request a full explanation, challenge the criterion, change a goal, pause or stop. Answer help is recorded, not moralized. No fixed learner profile, diagnostic label or ranking is generated.

## Exclusions

This is not psychotherapy, medical/legal/financial guidance, professional safety training, certification, student grading, employee assessment, or an autonomous tutor application. Do not use the package to override qualified professional judgment or institutional assessment rules. Physical skills may require in-person supervision that a chat cannot provide.

## Human learning and AI implementation

The scholarly library concerns human learning and instruction. AI use is a proposed application unless a specific study actually investigates it. The package does not claim access to hidden model reasoning, persistent memory, web browsing, automated reminders, audio analysis or code execution merely because a prompt requests them.

## Evidence conditions

Assisted task completion, immediate independent performance, delayed retention and changed-context application are recorded separately. Unknown intervals and missing responses remain unknown. A grade from the same AI that supplied the answer is not independent verification. Retain access accommodations while specifying answer-generating assistance.

## Research scope

The original source search was targeted to the five specialist Practices, not systematic or exhaustive. Selected academic sources span research designs and populations. Adolescent and classroom evidence has explicit indirectness for independent adults. Named published frameworks remain limited source-faithful summaries. Domain Models integrate identified contributions without claiming a newly validated theory of the learner or a causal model of an LLM.
