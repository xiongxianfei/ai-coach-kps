# Correct and Incorrect Algebra Examples

## Key takeaway

The experiments combined explanation of worked examples with guided algebra practice.

## Summary

This paper supplies a procedural basis for [Learning from Erroneous Examples](../methods/learning-from-erroneous-examples.md), not a general guarantee about learning from mistakes.

## Publication and inspection

[Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2012.11.002). [Inspected original-paper mirror](https://mrbartonmaths.com/resourcesnew/8.%20Research/Making%20the%20most%20of%20examples/Using%20example%20problems.pdf).

Section 3.1.2, pp. 26–27, describes already-labelled steps, supported explanations and available hints. The discussion, pp. 31–32, distinguishes conceptual outcomes from procedural results and retains the role of correct instruction and guided practice.

## Limits

The studied setting was school algebra with a Cognitive Tutor. A chat-based correction task changes the interaction and support. The article does not validate all error-diagnosis activities or transfer to adult singing, language or security learning. Bibliographic citations within it were not independently reviewed.

## Nonclaims

No original participant data, copyrighted figures, successful learner attempts, independent review or AI efficacy claim is included. The mirror is one copy of the publication, not an additional study.
