# Organizing Instruction and Study

## Key takeaway

The guide identifies instructional and study decisions with differing evidence ratings.

## Summary

The overview supplies topic coverage; selected full-guide passages add bounded guidance about learner readiness and changing support. Neither supplies a complete protocol for adult AI learning.

## Publication identity

Pashler and colleagues. 2007. *Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning*. Institute of Education Sciences, September 2007. [Official guide overview](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1). Selected passages were also inspected in the [official PDF](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/Docs/PracticeGuide/20072004.pdf).

## Inspected contribution

Recommendations address spacing, alternating demonstrations and problems, combining representations, retrieval, study judgments and explanatory questions. The page rates spacing and example alternation moderate, retrieval-based re-exposure and deep questions strong, and pre-questions and study-allocation recommendations minimal. These are the guide's ratings, not a new appraisal.

Printed pages 4 and 10 describe reducing example support and increasing independent problem solving as expertise develops, with guidance varying by learner and situation. Printed page 29 introduces deeper explanatory questions for learners with relevant foundational knowledge who are ready for more complex understanding. The master Practice's question-fit decisions apply these bounded ideas; the guide does not prescribe our four considerations or demonstrate their effect in adult AI coaching.

## Additional procedural inspection

Recommendation 2, printed pages 9–11, describes alternating worked solutions and learner problems, varying guidance with expertise, and gradually leaving more steps to the learner. It notes that added annotations are not universally helpful. This supports the bounded procedure in [Worked Example Instruction](../methods/worked-example-instruction.md); presenting all examples before all questions is a separate adaptation.

## Limits

Only the stated PDF passages and overview were inspected; other procedures and underlying studies need inspection before adopting further detailed prescriptions. Alternating worked examples with problems does not establish every claim about interleaving categories. School and postsecondary coverage does not automatically establish applicability to independent adult chatbot use.

## Nonclaims

No universal schedule, task size, visual-learning benefit or complete AI intervention is validated here. The guide and its figures are not redistributed.
