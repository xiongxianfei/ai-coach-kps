# Multimedia POE Tasks to Probe Understanding

## Key takeaway

The paper describes prediction with reasons, observation, and explanation of discrepancies in a classroom multimedia setting.

## Summary

This source supports the procedural basis of [Predict Observe Explain](../methods/predict-observe-explain.md). Its study of classroom perceptions does not establish learning effects in adult AI coaching.

## Publication and inspection

[Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1013106209449). [Inspected author-uploaded text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/227231359_Student_and_Teacher_Perceptions_of_the_Use_of_Multimedia_Supported_Predict-Observe-Explain_Tasks_to_Probe_Understanding).

The located passages describe the procedure (p. 590), an interpretive study of two secondary classes (p. 593), and paired use of video tasks (p. 594). Prediction formats included choices and drawings; the implementation also required written responses. Those format choices are not universal requirements of POE.

## Limits

The stated research focus was perceptions, discussion and eliciting views, not measuring conceptual change. White and Gunstone's book is cited within the paper but was not independently inspected for this contribution. The separate 2004 Kearney paper was not used as a procedural source after access failed.

## Nonclaims

No adult AI trial, causal effectiveness estimate, independent review or inspected bibliography is claimed. The author-uploaded rendering is the same publication, not corroborating evidence. No source media or full text is redistributed.
