# Guide to the After Action Review

## Key takeaway

Review expectations, actual events, useful actions and possible improvements after an episode of work.

## Summary

The [guide](https://aging.idaho.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Guide-to-the-After-Action-Review.pdf), version 1.1, supplies the four-part basis for [After Action Review](../methods/after-action-review.md).

## Located contribution

The Overview, printed page 1, describes a team review with open discussion, preservation of strengths and improvement recommendations. The authors' VA affiliations appear on the cover.

## Nonclaims

This is practitioner guidance, not a trial of individual learning or AI facilitation. The package's causal-uncertainty checks and assistance example are authored adaptations. The full guide and its report template are not copied; inaccessible passages are not represented as inspected.
