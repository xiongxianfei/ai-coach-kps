# Lectures

## Key takeaway

University lecture guidance provides concrete ways to organize explanatory teaching.

## Summary

The [CMU Eberly Center page](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/designteach/design/instructionalstrategies/lectures.html) contributes to [Structured Explanation](../methods/structured-explanation.md), an explicitly authored adaptation rather than a named CMU protocol.

## Located contribution

The opening ties lectures to suitable objectives. “Structuring your lecture” recommends orientation, transitions, developed examples with limits, summaries and a close. “Building interactivity into the lecture” offers learner tasks, questions and discussion.

## Nonclaims

This is professional teaching guidance, not a trial of the local conversational method. Attention-curve, attire and fixed-timing advice are not adopted. No claim that explanation alone establishes learning follows. The underlying cited books were not checked.
