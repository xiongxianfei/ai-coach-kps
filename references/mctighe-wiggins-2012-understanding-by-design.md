# Understanding by Design Framework

## Key takeaway

The framework links intended understanding and transfer to evidence and instructional planning.

## Summary

[McTighe and Wiggins's ASCD publication](https://files.ascd.org/staticfiles/ascd/pdf/siteASCD/publications/UbD_WhitePaper0312.pdf) supplies the procedural basis of [Backward Design](../methods/backward-design.md). This record does not appraise its effectiveness.

## Located contribution

Printed pages 1–2 introduce the planning approach and alignment. Pages 5–6 distinguish evidence from instructional planning, including culminating performances and other evidence. Page 7 FAQ 3 places individual lessons within larger units. The authors do not require every lesson to contain every facet of understanding.

## Nonclaims

The full framework, associated templates and research bibliography are not reproduced or certified here. The local statistics lesson is an authored application, not an ASCD example, endorsement or observed success. The source is copyright ASCD; no figures are copied.
