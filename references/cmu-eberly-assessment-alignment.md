# Align Assessments with Objectives

## Key takeaway

Learning objectives guide assessment design; an activity can serve different objectives.

## Summary

This institutional guidance informs the distinction between thinking and response format in the Instruction and Scaffolding Model.

## Publication identity

Eberly Center, Carnegie Mellon University, *Align Assessments with Objectives*, undated. [Inspected university page](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/designteach/design/assessments.html).

## Inspected contribution

The opening discussion links assessment choices to intended learning. The table illustrates revised Bloom categories through varied activities. Our question menu and examples are authored applications, not a reproduction of that table.

## Limits

This is teaching guidance, not a validation study of adult AI coaching. Inspection did not extend to the original revised taxonomy, linked resources or underlying research.

## Nonclaims

The source does not establish our question categories as exhaustive, prescribe a fixed teaching sequence or rank every question by difficulty. No source figures, learner data or complete assessment items are reproduced.
