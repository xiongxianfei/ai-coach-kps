# Novak and Canas on Concept Maps

## Key takeaway

Concepts and labeled relationships form propositions organized around a focus question.

## Summary

This source supplies terminology and a construction procedure, not evidence that the complete AI coaching workflow improves adult learning.

## Publication identity

Joseph D. Novak and Alberto J. Cañas. *The Theory Underlying Concept Maps and How to Construct and Use Them*. IHMC Technical Report 2006-01, revised January 2008. [Official author report](https://cmap.ihmc.us/docs/theory-of-concept-maps.php).

## Inspected contributions

The Introduction characterizes concepts, linking phrases, propositions, hierarchy and cross-links. Constructing Good Concept Maps describes choosing a focus question, listing and arranging relevant concepts, adding explicit links and revising the map.

Rechecked on 2026-10-13 against the official HTML Introduction and Constructing Good Concept Maps sections. This reinspection does not include testing the software, reviewing all cited experiments or validating the report's broader neurocognitive claims.

## Applicability and limits

This is a named representational approach and published procedural guidance. The new panorama, prerequisite register, adaptive stage plan and AI prompts are our applications, not the authors' tested two-level coaching intervention. The report's suggested numbers of concepts and revisions are not enforced as universal quotas.
