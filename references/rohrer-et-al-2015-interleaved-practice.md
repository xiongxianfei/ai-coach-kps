# Interleaved Practice Improves Mathematics Learning

## Key takeaway

The experiment supplies a mixed-problem procedure that requires strategy selection after instruction.

## Summary

The [ERIC-hosted article](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED557355.pdf) supports [Interleaved Practice](../methods/interleaved-practice.md). The [authors' summary](https://uweb.cas.usf.edu/~drohrer/pdfs/Rohrer_et_al_2015JEdPsychsummary.pdf) and [IES publication listing](https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/awards/interleaved-mathematics-practice) identify the final citation.

## Located contribution

The study used instructed mathematics problems, altered their arrangement and provided corrections. It tested seventh-grade students after a review. Because the arrangement also changes spacing, it does not isolate a pure discrimination mechanism.

## Nonclaims

The package's adult examples and error-analysis choices were not tested in this study. No universal mixing ratio, effect across all subjects or generic-chatbot benefit is inferred. No source figures, worksheets or raw data are bundled.
