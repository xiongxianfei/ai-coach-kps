# Performance Consultants GROW Guide

## Key takeaway

GROW connects a chosen goal and present circumstances to options and a learner-owned action.

## Summary

The [provider's guide](https://www.performanceconsultants.com/resources/the-grow-model/) supplies the procedural basis for [GROW Coaching](../methods/grow-coaching.md).

## Located contribution

The four stage descriptions identify Goal, Reality, Options and Will or Way Forward. The guide permits revisiting stages and cautions against leading the participant. Its promotional effectiveness claims are not adopted as empirical findings.
