# Predict Observe Explain Guidance

## Key takeaway

POE connects an initial expectation to an observable event and an explanation of the result.

## Summary

[NZCER's procedural guidance](https://arbs.nzcer.org.nz/predict-observe-explain-poe) supports preparation, sequencing and accessible alternatives in [Predict Observe Explain](../methods/predict-observe-explain.md).

## Located contribution

“What to do” puts prediction and its reason before the demonstration, followed by observation and a revised explanation. “Limitations” notes the difficulty of topics without readily observable outcomes and warns against making every demonstration a surprise. “Adapting the strategy” permits oral answers and supported choices.

## Limits

This is instructional guidance, not a new controlled experiment. Its suggested rationale does not establish that every prediction improves attention or that surprise necessarily changes understanding. Its bibliography was not independently inspected.

## Nonclaims

No new efficacy claim, AI implementation, independent review, classroom replication or source-media reproduction is made. Applicability to a particular adult task requires judgment.
