# Eberly Center Prior Knowledge Assessment

## Key takeaway

Sample relevant starting capabilities to inform instruction; separate performance evidence from self-report.

## Summary

The [overview](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/assessment/priorknowledge/) distinguishes direct and indirect measures. The [performance-based assessment page](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/assessment/priorknowledge/performancebased.html) supplies the procedural basis for [Baseline Check](../methods/baseline-check.md).

## Located contribution

The performance page begins with relevant background knowledge and skills, then tasks that sample them and instructional adjustment using the results. These checks serve diagnosis of preparation rather than course grading.
