# UNESCO Guidance on Generative AI

## Key takeaway

Educational AI guidance includes human agency, privacy and suitability for educational use.

## Summary

The official overview supports including these concerns in the knowledge outline. It is policy guidance, not a study of coaching effectiveness.

## Publication identity

Fengchun Miao and Wayne Holmes. 2023. *Guidance for generative AI in education and research*. UNESCO. [Official publication page](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/guidance-generative-ai-education-and-research).

## Inspected contribution

The landing page describes human-centered educational use, privacy protection, ethical validation, pedagogical design and institutional preparedness. These are coverage concerns for the interaction area; they do not supply a technical implementation.

## Limits

Only the overview was inspected. Detailed recommendations and jurisdiction-specific obligations require separate examination. The page's update date is not a new edition date.
