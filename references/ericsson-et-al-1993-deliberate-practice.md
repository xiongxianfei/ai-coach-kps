# Deliberate Practice and Expert Performance

## Key takeaway

The inspected account connects targeted improvement to suitable tasks, informative feedback and individualized training.

## Summary

The [article PDF](https://gwern.net/doc/psychology/writing/1993-ericsson.pdf) supplies the bounded basis for [Deliberate Practice Loop](../methods/deliberate-practice-loop.md). It is a third-party-hosted copy of the primary article; the masthead and passage pagination were inspected.

## Located contribution

Pages 367–368 discuss repeated attempts matched to prior knowledge and teacher-designed practice. They distinguish improvement-oriented training from ordinary work or play.

## Nonclaims

The local loop and AI implementation were not studied here. No expertise guarantee, universal hours requirement or claim that practice alone explains performance differences is adopted. This limited inspection does not resolve the wider theory or equate generic AI feedback with qualified coaching. No article text or figures are redistributed.
