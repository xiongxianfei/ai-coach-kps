## Meaning boundaries

A canonical name does not certify paraphrase faithfulness or effectiveness. Pieschl's calibration article is not attributed to Schraw; no generic loop is labeled Zimmerman's model. The published-model-subset relationship is an explicit domain adaptation for academic frameworks, not a standards conformance claim.
