# Retrieval Practice

## Key takeaway

Attempt to retrieve encountered material before returning to it.

## Summary

This is a procedural summary of the retrieval component in the inspected experiment, not an AI-specific protocol. Restudy, feedback and a later assessment are explicitly distinguishable.

## Inputs and prerequisites

Previously encountered material, a clear retrieval task and a reliable way to inspect the answer. A learner who has never encountered the material needs instruction, not repeated unsupported guessing.

## Rationale

Attempted retrieval can be a learning activity as well as an observation. Its evidential interpretation depends on what was available during the attempt.

## Procedure

1. Study the target material with its meaning available.
2. Put it aside and recall or reconstruct the relevant content.
3. Preserve the original response before returning to the material.
4. Inspect omissions and errors; distinguish the later correction from the original recall.
5. Repeat or assess later according to a specified plan, recording the actual condition and interval.

## Output and validation

An original recall response, a source comparison and a separate later check. In a constructed example, a learner states a distinction from memory, then discovers one missing qualification. This documents the attempt, not a guaranteed learning gain.

## Limits

The procedure is a basic summary, not an exact replication or universal schedule. Do not treat an AI answer revealed before the attempt as unaided recall. Evidence on comparison activities is qualified in Testing Effect.
