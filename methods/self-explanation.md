# Self Explanation

## Key takeaway

The learner generates an explanation connecting studied information to its meaning or justification.

## Summary

The inspected procedure elicited learner explanations after text segments. A KPS adaptation must distinguish those explanations from an AI-written solution.

## Inputs and prerequisites

A short studied passage or verified worked step, sufficient prior instruction, and a question that can be explained rather than only repeated.

## Rationale

The learning activity is generating the explanatory relation. Reading someone else's explanation is a different activity, even when the text is helpful.

## Procedure

1. Read a bounded segment or inspect a worked step.
2. Explain its meaning in relation to the preceding material or the relevant principle.
3. Make gaps and uncertain connections explicit rather than inventing a rationale.
4. Continue with clarification prompts as appropriate and preserve what the learner generated.

## Output and validation

A learner explanation. In a constructed calculation, the learner explains why dividing by quantity produces a per-unit amount. Whether the explanation is accurate requires a separate reference check; the original study is not a guarantee for this example.

## Limits

The selected procedure used school-age learners and a particular text. It does not prescribe a dialogue length, a fixed Socratic script or feedback timing for an AI coach.
