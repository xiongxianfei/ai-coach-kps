# Self Explanation Effect

## Key takeaway

Learner-generated explanation can support understanding under studied conditions.

## Summary

The selected source concerns learners producing explanations, not an AI producing more text for them. Its population and test conditions limit extrapolation.

## Statement

Chi and colleagues reported improved understanding for prompted self-explanation in their text-learning setting. The learner performed the explanatory activity; the benefit is not established for passively reading an AI rationale.

## Explanation and boundary

The inspected study involved eighth graders and differences in study time; the post-test allowed access to text and notes. Therefore it is not evidence of an exact no-help adult AI protocol or long-term retention outcome.

## Constructed application

After studying an example, the learner explains why a step is warranted and what would change under a different condition. A source check can reveal an invented justification. That checking safeguard is a Practice design choice, not the effect itself.

## Limits

Fluent, lengthy or confident explanations may still be wrong. No claim is made that every prompt or subject benefits equally.
