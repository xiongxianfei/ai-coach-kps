# Evidence to Practice map

## Key takeaway

A source-supported premise and an AI coaching application are different claims.

## Summary

Generated view of canonical package declarations. It is a navigation and review aid, not evidence of scientific validity or learner outcomes.

## Scope

Bounded claim records connect selected research with authored Practice decisions. This is a targeted map, not a systematic review, pooled effect-size analysis or causal model.

## Srl

**Source premise:** SRL is broader than metacognition alone.

[Source record](../references/panadero-2017-self-regulated-learning.md) — Abstract and Introduction.

**Population and context:** Review across educational models  
**Comparison:** Not an intervention comparison  
**Outcome and timing:** Conceptual scope; Not an effect estimate.

**Limit:** A review of theoretical frameworks does not test this AI coaching workflow. No particular Zimmerman model is reproduced or attributed from uninspected original text.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** Keep goal choices, monitoring and learner control visible; this stage sequence is our application.

## Performance

**Source premise:** Immediate performance and durable learning are distinct targets.

[Source record](../references/soderstrom-bjork-2015-learning-versus-performance.md) — Abstract.

**Population and context:** Integrative review; publisher abstract inspected  
**Comparison:** Acquisition performance versus retention and transfer evidence  
**Outcome and timing:** Distinction between observations; Depends on target; no fixed threshold.

**Limit:** Abstract-only inspection supports a narrow distinction, not specific optimal delays, universal mastery thresholds, or efficacy of an AI tutor.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** Separate assistance, timing and changed-context observations in records.

## Calibration

**Source premise:** Judgments and criterion evidence are distinct; calibration concerns their relationship.

[Source record](../references/pieschl-2009-metacognitive-calibration.md) — Abstract.

**Population and context:** Theoretical paper; abstract-only inspection  
**Comparison:** Traditional performance correspondence versus proposed extended account  
**Outcome and timing:** Conceptualization, not a local score; No empirical delay inferred.

**Limit:** The proposed extension is not a settled equivalence with every use of calibration. A single confidence rating or an AI statement cannot establish calibrated self-knowledge. This publication is by Pieschl, not Schraw.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** An optional confidence judgment should not replace a task response or become an automatic mastery label.

## Formative

**Source premise:** Quality standards, evaluative comparison and improvement moves support formative use.

[Source record](../references/sadler-1989-formative-assessment.md) — Abstract.

**Population and context:** Quality judgments using multiple criteria  
**Comparison:** Theoretical account; no local treatment effect  
**Outcome and timing:** Evaluative capacity and actionable improvement; Not a tested schedule.

**Limit:** Sadler explicitly gives the account less relevance to purely correct/incorrect responses. The AI adaptation and task-difficulty decision table are authored Practice choices, not his validated protocol.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** Check source correctness and act on one discrepancy; simple probes are a labeled extension.

## Scaffold

**Source premise:** Cognitive Apprenticeship relates modeled expertise, guided activity and learner participation.

[Source record](../references/collins-brown-holum-1991-cognitive-apprenticeship.md) — Method items 1 to 6; framework summary and concluding discussion.

**Population and context:** Instructional framework and illustrative settings  
**Comparison:** Not a generic-AI intervention comparison  
**Outcome and timing:** Published teaching relationships; No universal stage duration.

**Limit:** The authors do not provide one compulsory coaching script. A language model is not automatically an expert, and its explanation is not a verified trace of hidden cognition. No AI implementation is studied here.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** Verify AI examples; use contingent help and permit direct explanation without claiming expert cognition.

## Assessment

**Source premise:** Assessment relates cognition, observation and interpretation.

[Source record](../references/national-research-council-2001-assessment.md) — Chapter 2: Assessment as a Process of Reasoning from Evidence; The Assessment Triangle; relationships among the three vertices.

**Population and context:** Assessment framework across educational contexts  
**Comparison:** Not an intervention comparison  
**Outcome and timing:** Justified inference from selected observations; Defined by assessment purpose.

**Limit:** This representation does not supply subject-specific scoring rules or guarantee reliable automated grading. An interpretation remains uncertain and purpose-dependent.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** State target, task and interpretation before using an AI judgment.

## Feedback

**Source premise:** Feedback effects vary across contexts and informational forms.

[Source record](../references/wisniewski-zierer-hattie-2020-feedback.md) — Abstract and Introduction.

**Population and context:** Educational feedback studies summarized in the review  
**Comparison:** Multiple conditions, not one universal comparator  
**Outcome and timing:** Learning outcomes across included research; Heterogeneous; not extracted as one value.

**Limit:** This review does not validate AI-generated corrections, indicate that praise alone is diagnostic, or identify one universal feedback schedule. The definition is adopted from prior literature, not originated by these authors.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** Ask for inspectable criterion information and check actual uptake; do not presume AI feedback is correct.

## Retrieval

**Source premise:** The reported study–recall–restudy treatment supported later test outcomes in the inspected comparisons.

[Source record](../references/karpicke-blunt-2011-retrieval-practice.md) — Printed pages 772 to 773: retrieval condition, restudy, one-week short-answer test.

**Population and context:** Science-text experiments with students  
**Comparison:** Reported study and concept-mapping conditions; activities need separate accounting  
**Outcome and timing:** One-week short-answer and conceptual outcomes; One week in the inspected example.

**Limit:** The treatment includes more than recall alone. This is not evidence that retrieval beats every concept-mapping implementation; see the separate Mayrhofer comparison. Nor does it validate this package or a motor-learning task.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** Preserve original recall and correction; do not assert universal superiority.

## Retrieval Qualification

**Source premise:** Changing the comparison and memorization phase altered the superiority result.

[Source record](../references/mayrhofer-kuhbandner-frischholz-2023-testing-effect.md) — Abstract; Discussion on additional memorization and inference-question limits.

**Population and context:** Preregistered experiment reported by Mayrhofer and colleagues  
**Comparison:** Retrieval plus memorization versus mapping variants  
**Outcome and timing:** Verbatim and limited inference outcomes differ; One-week outcome context in the inspected discussion.

**Limit:** This questions a specific superiority claim, not all retrieval effects. The authors note the weakness of drawing broad conclusions from only two inference questions.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** Retain activity/time details rather than calling every benefit a retrieval-only effect.

## Spacing

**Source premise:** A favorable review gap depends on the retention interval in the inspected fact-learning account.

[Source record](../references/cepeda-2008-temporal-ridgeline.md) — Abstract.

**Population and context:** Fact-learning experiment; publisher abstract only  
**Comparison:** Different gaps and final-test delays  
**Outcome and timing:** Retention of learned material; Varied; not an optimal day recipe.

**Limit:** No fixed spacing algorithm, exact day sequence or individualized optimality is extracted from abstract-only inspection.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** Use a learner-owned revisable review plan and record actual intervals.

## Self Explanation

**Source premise:** Prompted learner explanations supported understanding in the selected text-learning experiment.

[Source record](../references/chi-1994-self-explanation.md) — Abstract; Procedure and explanation-elicitation prompts, printed pages 450 to 451.

**Population and context:** Eighth-grade learners; indirect for adults  
**Comparison:** Prompted explanations versus comparison reading conditions, with timing differences  
**Outcome and timing:** Understanding; post-test with text/notes available; Not taken as long-term independent retention.

**Limit:** Participants were eighth graders, not independent adult AI users. Conditions differed in time, and text/notes were available for the post-test. The contribution cannot be restated as proof of unaided adult retention from AI-generated explanations.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** Let the learner explain and check claims; an AI explanation is not learner self-explanation.

## Ai College

**Source premise:** A structured AI tutor improved reported short-term outcomes against the classroom comparator in that setting.

[Source record](../references/kestin-2025-ai-tutoring.md) — Discussion: Designing successful student-AI interactions; Context, limitations, and future directions; Methods.

**Population and context:** College introductory physics; custom source-informed tutor  
**Comparison:** In-class active learning in the reported randomized design  
**Outcome and timing:** Short-term topic-learning outcomes; Post-lesson; delayed learning not established.

**Limit:** The system used course-specific expert material and interaction controls. This does not establish long-term independent learning, general adult self-study effectiveness or equivalence to a generic chat prompt.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** Use verified material and observe actual tool behavior; a generic prompt is not the tested platform.

## Ai Secondary

**Source premise:** AI-assisted practice and subsequent no-access exam performance differed by assistance design.

[Source record](../references/bastani-2025-generative-ai-guardrails.md) — Abstract and Significance; footnotes on exam timing; published correction history.

**Population and context:** High-school mathematics; indirect for independent adults  
**Comparison:** Unrestricted interface, teacher-informed tutor safeguards and study control  
**Outcome and timing:** Assisted practice and no-access examination outcomes; Short-term exam, not long-term retention.

**Limit:** This is high-school mathematics, indirect evidence for independent adults. The exam is short-term, not a long-term retention measure. Teacher-supplied safeguards are not reproduced by a promise in a generic prompt. The inspected article notes a 2025 affiliation correction.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** Retain an independent check and source controls; do not generalize that AI always helps or harms.

## Concept Mapping

**Source premise:** Concept maps use explicit linking phrases to organize propositions around a focus question.

[Source record](../references/novak-canas-2008-concept-maps.md) — Introduction; Constructing Good Concept Maps.

**Population and context:** Author technical report on a knowledge representation and construction procedure  
**Comparison:** Not an intervention comparison  
**Outcome and timing:** Meaning and procedure, not a measured learning effect; No delay or developmental timescale tested here.

**Limit:** The report does not establish this AI workflow, an exhaustive panorama or prerequisite relationships. Theories and experiments elsewhere in the report were not evaluated.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** Use distinct panorama and goal-map artifacts, check important propositions, and keep instructional hypotheses separate. These are local design choices.

## Whole Task Orientation

**Source premise:** Cognitive Apprenticeship discusses global task orientation before component execution alongside complexity and diversity.

[Source record](../references/collins-brown-holum-1991-cognitive-apprenticeship.md) — Sequencing: Global before local skills; framework summary.

**Population and context:** Published instructional framework with varied educational examples  
**Comparison:** Not an experiment on adult AI-coach domain panoramas  
**Outcome and timing:** An instructional sequencing account; No optimum preliminary survey duration is prescribed.

**Limit:** Whole-task understanding is not the same as a whole-discipline map. The article does not require exhaustive orientation or validate this sequence.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** Offer a bounded panorama before detailed planning and revisit the whole when a local relation changes; preserve learner choice.

## Learning Progression Boundary

**Source premise:** The NRC science account treats learning progressions as research-informed descriptions of increasingly sophisticated understanding, including multiple paths.

[Source record](../references/national-research-council-2007-learning-progressions.md) — Chapter 8, printed page 214 and Key Characteristics on pages 220 to 222.

**Population and context:** K–8 science and developmental/instructional research synthesis  
**Comparison:** Conceptual description, not a trial of adult AI coaching  
**Outcome and timing:** A research use of the term learning progression; Extended schooling rather than one chat session.

**Limit:** This school-science account is not direct evidence for an adult topic plan. An authored order does not acquire empirical validation from a label.

**Authored application, not a demonstrated effect:** Call the local output a provisional instructional sequence and record the evidence needed to revise it.
