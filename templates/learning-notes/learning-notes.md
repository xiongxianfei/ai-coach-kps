# Learning notes

Replace these prompts with your own notes. Adapt the headings and detail as useful; keep personal notes in a private location. The frontmatter above can be removed from your private copy.

## Goal

What do I want to understand or do? Add preferences and constraints that affect the learning.

## Starting point

Keep my initial self-assessment and any actual baseline response here, with its date, task and help conditions. Distinguish what I reported from what I demonstrated. Leave unknown capabilities not assessed; preserve this starting point as learning continues.
