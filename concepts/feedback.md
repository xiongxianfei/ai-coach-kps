# Feedback

## Key takeaway

Feedback supplies information about performance or understanding; delivery alone does not establish improvement.

## Summary

The inspected educational-feedback review adopts an informational definition and documents heterogeneous effects. The definition does not certify an AI evaluator.

## Definition and distinctions

Feedback concerns information used to relate a response or process to a target. Information about the work differs from praise of the person. A revision and subsequent check are different observations from receiving a comment.

## Constructed example

"Good job" supplies little basis for checking a calculation. "Your denominator is the number of groups rather than the number of objects; explain that choice" identifies something inspectable. The second comment is still only a proposal until checked against a valid task criterion.

## Scope and limits

The review does not show that every detailed correction helps or that immediate feedback is always best. Truth, relevance, learner interpretation and opportunity to act all need separate examination in AI use.
