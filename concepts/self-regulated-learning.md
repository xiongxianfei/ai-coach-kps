# Self Regulated Learning

## Key takeaway

Self-regulated learning includes monitoring and control of learning as well as motivational, affective and behavioral dimensions.

## Summary

The academic construct is broader than managing a study schedule or reporting confidence. Panadero provides the inspected field characterization; no single uninspected named model is substituted for it.

## Definition and distinctions

Self-regulated learning concerns how learners direct and adapt their activity toward learning goals. The inspected review includes cognitive, metacognitive, behavioral, motivational and affective dimensions. It is not synonymous with metacognition, solitary learning, or compliance with an AI plan.

## Constructed example

An adult chooses a small programming goal, checks an attempted solution, notices an unresolved prerequisite, asks for a different example and revises the next practice task. The observable choices can be logged; a conversational description alone does not establish a psychological capacity.

## Scope and limits

Different SRL theories organize these processes differently. This Concept supplies vocabulary, not a universally ordered phase model. The learner's informed decisions remain distinct from the AI's proposed adaptations.
