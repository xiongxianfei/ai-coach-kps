# Source and author review

## Key takeaway

Inspection of research, authoring review and independent validation are separately reported.

## Summary

This is a targeted, provisional synthesis for four adult AI coaching tasks. Sources were selected for specific constructs and decisions; this was not an exhaustive systematic search or meta-analysis.

## Source access

The [source register](references/README.md) records actual inspection. Selected original material was inspected for Cognitive Apprenticeship, the NRC Assessment Triangle, retrieval procedures and self-explanation procedures. Several sources were available only as abstracts; those claims remain deliberately narrow. Pieschl's calibration article is correctly attributed to Stephanie Pieschl with citation year 2009 and first-online year 2008.

Original Zimmerman material was not adequately accessible in this run; no Zimmerman Model was manufactured from a review. A detailed Distributed Practice Method was not admitted from abstract-only access; the basic temporal design is a labeled application inside a Practice. Core's source-alignment test was adapted to this domain rather than copied with its Core-specific migration assertions.

## Conflicting and indirect evidence

The retrieval superiority claim is qualified by Mayrhofer and colleagues' altered comparison and outcome limitations. Adult college physics findings and adolescent mathematics findings differ in settings, controls and timing. The source descriptions retain these distinctions rather than average them into one AI-coaching effectiveness score. Teacher-informed solutions and platform guardrails are not equivalent to an instruction in a chat.

## What was author reviewed

The authoring assistant checked local explanation, source identity, role classification, prompt boundaries, source-to-application statements, original versus corrected responses, and the links used in each Practice. The definitions and Practice summaries were compared before the initial dependency baseline was established. This is an authoring review, not an independent scholar's approval.

## What remains untested

No real adult learner used these Practices under observation; no live model session, behavior-enforcement test, delayed retention study, transfer study, usability trial, accessibility audit or causal comparison was performed. No latent learner diagnosis or actual memory trace is inferred. The record checker validates declarations only.

## Next review tasks

Prioritize expert review of AI-specific adaptation, appropriate assessments for each target subject, missing full-text access, learner-agency tradeoffs and real usability. Test one bounded Practice with consent before scaling. Publish observed outcomes only under appropriate rights and privacy conditions, and keep them distinct from the scientific library.
